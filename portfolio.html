about.me

Quality isn't a phase. It's a habit.

I approach every product like a first-time user and a determined skeptic at the same time. My job is to find the problems before your customers do — and to report them so clearly that they get fixed fast.

I specialize in manual testing across web and mobile, API validation with Postman, and building clear, reproducible bug reports. I care about coverage, communication, and shipping software teams can trust.

// testing philosophy

"A test that never fails isn't proving quality — it's just checking a box. I test to learn where things break, not to confirm they work."

Frequently asked

What types of testing do you offer?+
Manual functional, regression, smoke, sanity, UI, cross-browser and responsive testing for web and mobile, plus API testing with Postman. If you're not sure what you need, I'll help you scope it.
Do you write automated tests?+
My core strength is manual and API testing with clear, reproducible reporting. For automation needs I can advise on strategy and tooling, or collaborate with your automation engineers.
How do you deliver bug reports?+
In your tool of choice — Jira, ClickUp, Trello, or a shared sheet — with steps to reproduce, expected vs. actual behavior, severity, and screenshots or recordings as evidence.
How can we start working together?+
Reach out through the contact form below, my Fiverr gig, or LinkedIn. Share your product and goals, and I'll suggest the right testing approach and timeline.
What's your turnaround time?+
It depends on scope, but I always agree on a clear timeline up front. Small audits can be same-week; larger cycles are planned around your release schedule.
